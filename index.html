    CO2 vs VOCs

    CO2 is easy. Benzene is hard.

    Ventilation fixes CO2. It doesn't always fix what off gasses from furniture, stoves and cleaning products, and a cheap TVOC sensor can't tell you which compound it is.

    800 ppm

    CO2: sense it, ventilate it.

    Every 500 ppm rise in CO2 is linked to roughly a 15% drop in cognitive scores. The autopilot keeps occupied rooms under target automatically.

    3 µg/m³

    Benzene: alarm, then diagnose.

    The LEED v5 benzene limit. We treat the sensor like a smoke alarm and the lab like the doctor.

    1. Sensors catch a VOC spike that ventilation doesn't clear.
    2. CL.AIR flags the room and recommends a targeted lab test.
    3. Results land in the LEED report, with the fix logged.
